# Car-Sales-Profit-Analysis-PowerBI
Interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing car rental sales, profit, bookings, customer types, booking channels, fuel types, car types, cities, and monthly sales trends.




🚗 Car Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard – Power BI
📊 Project Overview

The Car Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Microsoft Power BI dashboard designed to analyze car rental/sales performance and profitability across different cities, car types, customer types, booking channels, fuel types, and booking patterns.

The dashboard transforms raw car rental/sales data into meaningful visual insights that can help businesses understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and booking trends.

🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

Analyze total car sales and total profit.
Track total and accepted bookings.
Compare sales and profit across different car types.
Analyze sales based on customer type.
Compare online and offline booking performance.
Analyze sales and profit by fuel type.
Understand weekday and weekend booking patterns.
Monitor monthly sales and profit trends.
Compare business performance across different cities.
Build an easy-to-use interactive dashboard for business analysis.
Present important KPIs and insights in a visually appealing format.
